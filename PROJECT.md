## Audience

## Goal
Support job applications, present engineering experience and projects, and invite professional inquiries.

## Brand
Kevin C. Mendoza. Professional identity: Electronics Engineer and Electronics Technician.

## Visual Direction
Precision-engineering editorial direction: warm light-neutral canvas, deep navy, restrained cobalt accents, Manrope display/body typography, IBM Plex Mono only for technical metadata, generous whitespace, clear typographic hierarchy, fine dividers, and a custom geometric circuit-board hero illustration. Concept-led project cards with no project or stock photos; structured, categorized technical expertise rather than generic skill bars. Responsive, accessible, recruiter-first.

## Page Structure
Hero with contact and résumé actions; professional highlights; about; internship experience; selected projects; technical capabilities; education and credentials; contact form; footer.

## Copy Direction
Direct first-person writing. Prioritize recruiter-facing qualifications and hands-on engineering work. Distinguish documented experience from project contributions.

## Assets
Web-ready résumé PDF `Mendoza_Kevin_Resume.pdf` is committed to the repository root and linked from the hero section. User prefers visual project-card layouts without pictures. Keep project cards text/concept-led; do not add supplied project images or stock imagery. Technical expertise should be organized into clear categories with tools and capabilities. Describe project objectives, concepts, tools, and known contributions without inventing technical specifications or outcomes.

## Functionality
Responsive single-file HTML page. Contact form opens a prefilled email draft to kevincaceresmendoza@gmail.com; it does not submit to a backend. LinkedIn, email, phone, and résumé links are included. The live deployment URL is recorded above.

## Avoid
Generic portfolio layouts, excessive animation, flashy colors, clutter, and unsupported project claims. Do not publish a residential street address.
